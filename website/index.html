Why CONECTR

Offline apps you can trust, without losing your data.

ExoSynk Labs' apps never ask for internet access. That keeps your data on your phone — and leaves it there when your phone is lost, reset or replaced. CONECTR is the separate, optional app that solves that, so the apps themselves never have to go online.

The app stays offline

XPENC has no internet permission and never will. CONECTR is a different app that does the online part for it.

Only the backup file

CONECTR reads and writes the backup XPENC exports to a shared folder. It never opens the app's own database.

Every phone in sync

Back up on one phone, and your other phones pull it down. XPENC takes it in by itself.

Features

Careful with your data, by design.

Never overwrites newer data

Like git push: a phone may only replace a backup it already has. A stale phone can't wipe out another phone's newer entries.

Conflict-safe

Two phones backing up at the same second? The server accepts one; the other simply tries again. Nothing is lost.

Background auto-sync

After every XPENC backup, daily or weekly — and newer backups from your other phones are checked about hourly.

Friends & shared entries

Add friends by username and keep a shared "who owes whom" ledger that's the same on both phones. Every entry is accepted by the other side.

Sign in your way

Google, a one-time email code, or GitHub. Tokens stay in Android's encrypted storage.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license. Releases are built and signed by GitHub Actions, with checksums.

Privacy & security

Your data isn't our business model.

No ads. No analytics. No selling. Here's exactly where things go.

End-to-end encrypted ExoCloud

Each backup is sealed on your phone with AES-256-GCM under a random 256-bit key. The key lives only on your phones and in the 52-character recovery key you save. ExoSynk stores ciphertext it cannot open.

Least access to Google

CONECTR asks only for drive.file: it can see the files it created and nothing else in your Drive. Drive data goes straight between your phone and Google.

Locked-down accounts

Every account's rows in Supabase are protected by row-level security. Friends see only what you share with them; settings are yours alone.

Verifiable releases

Each APK is built from the public source by GitHub Actions, signed with CONECTR's release key, and published with a SHA-256 checksum.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is CONECTR free?

Yes. CONECTR is free and open source. Google Drive and GitHub backups use your own accounts. ExoCloud is free during beta (your newest 10 backups, up to 10 MB each); a paid tier with more room may come later.

Can ExoSynk Labs read my data?

No. Drive and GitHub backups live in your own accounts. ExoCloud backups are encrypted on your phone with a key we never receive.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Install CONECTR on your new phone, sign in, and pull your latest backup — XPENC takes it in. With ExoCloud, you'll need your recovery key.

What if I lose my ExoCloud recovery key?

Any phone that's still signed in can show it again in Settings → Backup storage. If every phone and the key are gone, the encrypted backups can't be opened by anyone — that's what end-to-end means. Keep it in a password manager.

Why a separate app instead of syncing inside XPENC?

So XPENC can promise it never goes online. Everything that needs the internet lives in CONECTR, which you can install — or not.

Is it on the Play Store?

Not yet. Download the signed APK from GitHub Releases; every release lists a SHA-256 checksum to verify it.
